YouTube reply desk

Privacy policy

The YouTube reply desk is a private tool for American Hairline and Alchemane staff. It reads comments on those two YouTube channels, drafts a reply, and posts or hides a comment only when a person chooses that action.

Signing in with Google gives the desk access to those YouTube channels. The desk stores the comment, the draft reply, and the channel it belongs to so staff can review it. It does not sell this information and it does not use it for advertising.

To ask for a copy or deletion of data held by the desk, email americanhairline.info@gmail.com.
